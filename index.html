About Us – Swanky Foundation Children’s Home

At Swanky Foundation Children’s Home, we are more than just a shelter—we are a family. Our mission is to provide a safe, loving, and nurturing environment for vulnerable, orphaned, and abandoned children, helping them heal, grow, and thrive.

Who We Are

Swanky Foundation Children’s Home is a non-profit, community-based organization dedicated to rescuing and supporting children in need. We believe that every child deserves to grow up in a secure environment where they are protected, valued, and given the opportunity to reach their full potential.

What We Do

  • Shelter & Basic Needs: Safe accommodation, nutritious meals, and clean clothing.
  • Education: School enrollment, tutoring, and mentorship support.
  • Health Care: Medical checkups, treatment, and emotional support.
  • Emotional & Spiritual Support: Counseling and spiritual guidance.
  • Life Skills & Talent Development: Music, art, sports, and vocational training.

Our Approach

We run like a true family. Every child is treated with love and dignity. We collaborate with social workers, guardians, and donors to ensure each child’s well-being. Transparency and accountability are at the heart of what we do.

Our Vision

To raise a generation of responsible, educated, and empowered children who will become agents of change in their communities.

Our Mission

To rescue, protect, and raise children in a loving, faith-based home, equipping them with the tools they need to live fulfilling and independent lives.

Our Projects

1. Feeding Program

Our children receive nutritious meals daily. We also reach out to children in surrounding communities with food support during outreaches.

2. Talent and Skills Development

We nurture children’s talents in music, art, sports, and vocational skills like tailoring, beadwork, and farming to prepare them for life ahead.

3. Rescue and Shelter Program

In partnership with community leaders, we rescue orphaned or abandoned children and give them a safe, loving home to grow in.

4. Future Dreams Project

We aim to build a fully equipped children’s village with dormitories, classrooms, a clinic, and a playground to accommodate more children.
